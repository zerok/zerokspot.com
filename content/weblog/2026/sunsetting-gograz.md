title: Sunsetting GoGraz
---

The 10th anniversary of [GoGraz](https://gograz.org/) has also been its last in its current setup. Right after the last meeting in [September 2025](https://gograz.org/meetup/2025-09-15/) some things happened in my life that required my full attention and that made GoGraz unfortunately irrelevant. I wanted to wait and see if things could get back to normal again but a couple of weeks ago Dorian and I met and decided it was finally time to end GoGraz.

It's been interesting 10 years. We never got the meetup to the same level as [PyGraz](https://pygraz.org/) but it was still a lot of fun! I also want to once again thank Dorian for all his energy and patience of all these years! Without him, GoGraz would have ended much sooner 🙂

If you're interested in continuing GoGraz, please get in touch and we can talk!
